### Listing vs. listing
In McGill's subreddit, Course Info and Exams is over-represented in the 
/new while Social is over-represented in /top. The same pattern can 
be found in Concordia's subreddit, although Course Info and Exams is 
also over-represented in Concordia's /top. The /top selects the posts 
that were interacted with the most, so it makes sense for Social to 
be very represented here. Meanwhile, the course information and exams 
are posted during the semester and right after exams so it makes sense 
that these threads are more recent.

### Does the comparison survive?
If you only has the /new data for both McGill and Concordia, you would 
see that Concordia has 9x more Social posts than McGill. While 
Concordia also has more Social posts in the /top, the difference is 
nowhere near as shocking. Overall, McGill and Concordia have a similar 
distribution for the different categories, except that McGill has much 
more Advice and Motivation posts than Concordia. Concordia makes up for 
this, with their extra Social posts.

### Random, but random of what?
we collected a random sample of the first 100 entries for each of the 
sections (McGill/Concorida and new/top). Reddit favors more recent and 
high performing posts when it limits the entries to 100 so this is not 
representative of the entire McGill or Concordia subreddit. To get 
closer to this, we would want to collect every post in each subreddit. 
This way, when we generate random numbers to pick our sample, we are 
sampling from the entire population of posts and not a select few.

### How much should we trust this?
We can not trust this because we have not looked at enough data to be 
sure that this is representative of the entire subreddit. For example 
our current sample shows that neither the Concordia /new or /top have 
any posts about Housing. If we were to assume that the sample is 
representative of the entire subreddit, then we would think that no one 
at Concordia is looking for housing or needs help with their housing. I 
don't think this is true since many Concordia students are in the same 
boat as McGill students when it comes to housing. It does not make 
sense to assume that there are 0 people at Concordia looking for 
housing, so if we expanded our sample size, we would begin to see more 
Concordia students with Housing posts.
