## What is the difference between found and designed data
Found data is data that is collected from various activities but is 
reused to analyze to help solve a certain problem. Designed data is 
data that is generated with the goal of answering a specific question 
in mind.

## What are the two primary challenges that necessitate sampling when collecting data
### 1. Not enough resources to get the entire population
Getting information from the entire population can be very costly 
and/or take a lot of time which you may not have. Sampling is a way to 
get around that.

### 2. Practically impossible to access the entire population
Some populations are very hard to track because they are constantly 
changing or are very hard to access. In this case, sampling a smaller 
portion of the population also becomes helpful.

## Why are website owners more likely to be upset about collecting data using scrapers than using API's
API's follow the website owner's terms and can potentially generate 
revenue for them, while scraping their website bypasses all of that.
