# Analysis
The most common complaint in the early months (January and February) is HEAT/HOT WATER. Heating is a big problem during the winter when it is cold outside.
During the summer months (June and July), it is no longer import to have heating in your home because it is already hot outside, in fact many people will
be trying to cool their homes. The difference in temperatures in these different months is responsible for the large discrepancy in counts of complaints between
the early winter months and the summer months, from 80,000+ to less than 10,000.
