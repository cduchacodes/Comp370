## 1.
* You can access a cloud machine from anywhere, but you need to have 
  your laptop with you
* It's more secure
* A cloud machine offers much more memory and compute than you could 
  get on your laptop, and it's easy to scale it up as well, if needed
* You don't need to worry about maintenance

## 2.
**Question Definition** - Clearly stating the question that you are trying 
to solve.  

**Data collection** - Collect the raw information that is needed to solve 
the problem.  

**Data annotation** - Generate features that will be help with data 
analysis.  

**Data analysis** - Create statistical models and visualizations using the 
data from before.  

**Interpretation** -  Use the models and visualizations to make 
inferences about the data.

**Communication** - Share the findings with others.

## 3.
Data analysts use data to get a new insight on something, while data 
scientists use the data science process to measure something that has 
never been measured before.

## 4. 
At any point in the data science process, you may want to go back and 
revise the question. This could be because you found out your question 
wasn't precise enough, or you weren't focussing on the right thing. You 
could also go back to get more data that you didn't think you would 
need at first. If your analysis doesn't make sense or something is 
missing, it is always possible to go back and go through the process 
again, causing a loop.
